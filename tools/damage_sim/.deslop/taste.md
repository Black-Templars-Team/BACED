# User taste

## Screen roles

- Damage simulator: this page exists to show what one hit (and a short burst) from a chosen weapon does to a Space Marine player (wounds, severity, and whether he goes down) and why, step by step. It is not the page for editing mod configs or comparing every weapon at once. (2026-11-25)

- Weapon balancing tab: compares every cert and relic weapon at once, a deliberate exception to the single-weapon role above. The target and aim stay in the shared left column (2026-11-26).

## Choices

- Lead answer: hits to unconscious / fatal (burst curve) first, single-hit wounds after (2026-11-25).
- Black Carapace armor mode is a visible toggle, not a hidden setting: it changes the whole damage chain (2026-11-25).
- Look: plain instrument panel, light and dark following the system, one accent reserved for danger (2026-11-25).
- Output: local file plus private artifact (2026-11-25).
- Arrangement A: every input in a left column in task order (weapon, ammo, range, body figure, loadout, Black Carapace), results on the right (2026-11-25).
- Tunables (ACE settings, Black Carapace values, item armor, ammo values, engine assumptions) sit inline next to the step of the chain that uses them, not in a drawer or tab (2026-11-25).

- Impact picker: a real front view of the marine with a clickable aim point and the shot cloud, not a generic diagram (2026-11-25).
- Burst length is whole magazines of the selected ammo, not a free hit count (2026-11-25).
- The chart must say plainly that each line is a running chance ("by shot X, Y% of bursts"), with an optional smoothing toggle (2026-11-25).
- End result shown as a replica of ACE's medical menu (body image, colours, fractures, injury list wording), even with a human body (2026-11-25).
- Page defaults come from the BT server's cba_settings export (2026-11-25).

- Balancing plot: cert colours from the validated categorical slots, generation as shape (circle Firstborn, diamond Primaris, triangle Reliquary), several certs as equal pie slices, other mods as hollow rings (2026-11-26).
- Balancing axes: X time to down, Y picker defaulting to consistency; replaces shots vs time (2026-11-26)

## Rejections / vetoes

- No 40k ornament or gothic theme (2026-11-25).
