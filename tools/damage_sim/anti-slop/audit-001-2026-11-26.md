# antislop audit 001: Weapon balancing tab (2026-11-26)

Mode: after (review requested, nothing changed). Evidence: `balance.js`, `damage_model.js` (`shotsToKill`,
`fastestMode`, `timeToShot`), and the page loaded headless at 1360 px in the dark theme with default inputs
(Player, Mk 7, chest, 100 m, 2 mrad, suit off), 284 pairs, 252 marks plotted. The 390 px layout and the light theme
were not re-checked in this audit.

Priority: HIGH = Hard Gate, MEDIUM = Purpose-Gate, LOW = Quality Lock. Fix nothing until numbers are approved.

| # | Pri | Rule | Finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | HIGH | C-5 / R-38 | Y is computed from X: `ttk = (n-1)*interval + reloads*R`. The chart presents two measures, but it is really "shots" against "shots divided by fire rate". 33 marks at x = 16 differ in Y only by rpm. |
| 2 | HIGH | C-5 / R-38 | Overplotting hides data: 26 pairs sit at exactly (1 shot, 0 s) and render as one mark. Columns at x = 2, 3 and 16 stack too. Hover picks one nearest mark, so the rest can only be reached through the table. |
| 3 | HIGH | C-5 / R-38 | Identical labels for different classes. `[Astartes] Missile Launcher` with Krak appears at 1 shot and at 128 shots. The second is `TIOW_SM_Rocket_1_LSLOT`: FullAuto mode, 50 mrad dispersion. `Heavy Bolt Pistol (BT)` / `12rnd Bolt Pistol Mag` appears twice (`Taj_Heavy_Bolt_Pistol` and `_Secondary`). Readers cannot tell these apart. |
| 4 | HIGH | C-5 | Internal names leak into the tooltip and table: the fire mode reads `this · 750 rpm`, and the mode label on a 0 s interval reads `Single · single shot`. |
| 5 | HIGH | C-2 / R-26 | Ammo and magazine overrides are deleted before the balance run (`balance.js:124`), so value edits in the Single weapon tab silently do not apply on this tab. That breaks the "what if I change this round" workflow. |
| 6 | HIGH | C-5 | Outliers that look like artifacts (Castellan missile 466 shots / 924 s on 4 launchers, LSLOT Krak 1581 s, shotgun pellets 235 shots) set the axis range. They squeeze about 90% of the marks into the bottom-left quarter. |
| 7 | HIGH | R-39 | The chart SVG has `role="img"` but is focusable and takes arrow keys, Enter and Escape. That ARIA contradicts the interactive behaviour. |
| 8 | HIGH | C-5 | Legend counts (Tactical 91, Intercessor 75 ...) include pairs that are never downed or not yet computed, so they do not match the marks drawn. |
| 9 | MEDIUM | R-06 | Tracked uppercase headings (`h2` 0.08em uppercase: "WEAPON BALANCING", "FIRSTBORN", "PRIMARIS") repeat the tab name and add no hierarchy. |
| 10 | LOW | R-29 | 11 cert categories cycle through 7 colour slots, so Tactical = Intercessor, Devastator = Heavy Intercessor and Assault = Jump Pack. At 5.5 px only the shape separates them, and split pie marks are unreadable at that size. |
| 11 | LOW | R-31 | Three control styles in one row: segmented (kill), number field (reload) and pills (acquisition). The pills have no reason to differ from the segmented control. |
| 12 | LOW | C-1 | The aim figure in the shared left column still shows the shot cloud of the Single weapon tab's weapon, which is hidden on this tab. |
| 13 | LOW | C-3 | "Data and settings" sits under the chart with "Reset 0 changed values" enabled. The table, the most precise view of 284 rows, is collapsed at the bottom. |
| 14 | LOW | C-5 | The intro says "Further right needs more hits". X counts shots fired, misses included. |
